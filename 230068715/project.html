Campus Residence Maintenance Reporting System

Project Description

Campus residences often face slow, informal, or untracked maintenance reporting processes, resulting in delayed repairs, poor communication between residents and maintenance staff, and a lack of accountability. The Campus Residence Maintenance Reporting System (CRMRS) aims to address this by providing a centralized, web-based platform where students can submit and track maintenance complaints, and maintenance staff can receive, manage, and resolve them efficiently. The system is designed to improve transparency, response times, and overall communication between residents and maintenance teams.
